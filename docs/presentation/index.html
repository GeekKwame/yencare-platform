Clinical Governance

Fair Access & Production Guardrails

Strict business logic guarantees equal access, eliminates appointment scalping, and preserves clinic throughput.

Rule 1 • Anti-Hoarding HTTP 409 Conflict
Student 2-Booking Cap
Students cannot hold more than 2 active non-completed appointments concurrently. Rejects hoarding attempts while allowing genuine follow-up visits.
Rule 2 • Concurrency Lock Atomic Update
Atomic Slot Reservation
Zero double-booking guarantee. Employs conditional atomic MongoDB queries with partial unique indexes on (clinicianId, date, time).
Rule 3 • Arrival Window -60m / +15m Window
Dynamic Arrival Window
Self-service arrival check-in is restricted to 60 min early to 15 min late. Late students are gently routed to reception triage for manual reassignment.
Rule 4 • Slot Recycling Automated Cron
No-Show Slot Recycling
Unattended visits past the grace period are marked as NO_SHOW, clearing the clinician queue and releasing the slot immediately for emergency walk-ins.
Security & Compliance

Enterprise Security & Anti-Abuse Hardening

Protecting student confidentiality, clinical integrity, and preventing unauthorized identity modification or enumeration.

🛡️
Anti-Brute-Force Rate Limiting
4-digit reference codes are protected by layered rate limiters: 30 req/min for general lookups and strict 5 attempts / 15 min on arrival check-ins.
🔑
SMS OTP Verification
Cancelling or rescheduling an appointment requires a one-time 4-digit code dispatched to the verified phone on record, preventing malicious tampering.
🩺
Doctor Queue Isolation
Workstation endpoints derive clinician identity exclusively from cryptographically signed JWTs, isolating patient queues strictly to the attending physician.
👁️
Phone & Privacy Masking
Public lookups and corridor displays mask student phone numbers (e.g., 053****884), ensuring compliance with Ghana Data Protection Act (Act 843).
🧹
NoSQL Injection Sanitizer
Generic recursive middleware strips dangerous MongoDB operators ($gt, $regex, $where) from incoming query and body payloads.
📜
Immutable Audit Trail
Every status change, override, cancellation, and check-in logs an immutable audit event tracking the acting actor (patient, reception, or clinician).
